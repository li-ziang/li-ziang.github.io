I am a third-year CS PhD student at School of Computational Science and Engineering (CSE), [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/), advised by Prof. [Yunan Luo](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~yunan/). I have broad interests in computational biology and machine learning, with a particular focus on developing AI methods to aid real-world biological and medical research. Much of my work has been about understanding and engineering proteins — from how mutations shape their stability and function, to more recently how proteins fold, move, and interact in cells. I dream of one day using machine learning to design molecular machines and programmable structures inspired by microtubules and the cellular environments that shape them.


Previously, I won a gold medal in Chemistry Olympiad during high school and obtained a B.E. in CS from Tsinghua University. I was honored to have worked with Prof. [Jie Tang](https://keg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/jietang/) at Tsinghua University and Prof. [Maria Brbic](https://cs.stanford.edu/~mbrbic/), Prof. [Jure Leskovec](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/jure/) at Stanford. In the industry, I used to intern at Tencent Quantum Lab, working with Prof. [Chang-Yu Hsieh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K-AjhSgAAAAJ&hl=id&oi=ao) and at [Helixon](https://www.helixon.com/), working with Prof. [Jian Peng](https://jianpeng.web.engr.illinois.edu/) and Prof. [Jianzhu Ma](https://majianzhu.com/).

My first name is pronounced like “Zion”.
